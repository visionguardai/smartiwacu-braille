Smartiwacu Support

Improve image recognition

  • Photograph the Braille page straight on and keep the full page in frame.
  • Use even side lighting that makes the dots visible without harsh glare.
  • Avoid shadows, blur, folded paper, and a steep camera angle.
  • Review uncertain cells before using the translated text.

Report a problem

Open an issue in the public Smartiwacu Braille repository. Describe the browser, what you expected, and what happened. Do not post private documents or personal information in a public issue.
